PaintFlow Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

PaintFlow is a paint and image annotation app for macOS made by Aiden Azarnoush.

Data collection

PaintFlow does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services.

Network access

PaintFlow does not connect to the internet. All images you open, edit, and save stay on your Mac.

Files

The app only reads and writes the image files you choose to open or save through the standard macOS file dialogs, or that you paste from the clipboard. It does not access any other files.

Purchases

If you purchase PaintFlow, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the Mac App Store. Aiden Azarnoush does not receive your payment details.

Children

PaintFlow does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through LinkedIn or the contact details on aiden-azarnoush.github.io.
